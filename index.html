How to use SpellSeekr

Searching

  • Describe what you need: cheap removal in black, blue instants that draw a card. Dutch works with the access code.
  • Cheap = mana value 2 or less, expensive = 6 or more. For price: budget (under €2) or under 1 euro.
  • Know Scryfall's syntax? Start with /s: /s t:elf mv=1. Only Commander-legal paper cards are shown.
  • To change the results instead of searching again, switch the search bar from New to Refine (or start with +): only instants, under 1 euro.
  • The chips under the heading show what your words were translated into. Take one out with its × to widen the search.
  • Filters & sort narrows the results by type, mana value and price. Card size and cards per page are under Settings.
  • With the access code you can ask for combos: 2 card combos in dimir.

Commander

  • Set it on the start screen: every search stays in its colors. A color outside them is left out (in red).
  • Partner, Background or Doctor? + add partner adds the second one.
  • A Companion adds its deck rule to every search, e.g. Lurrus: permanents of 2 mana or less.
  • Every card in the results also shows how many of its decks play it.

Your list and deck

  • Click a card to add it (a mistake? Undo), click again to remove it. List (top right) shows it grouped by type, with a mana curve.
  • Copy decklist puts it on the clipboard for Moxfield, Archidekt and other deck sites.
  • Import deck marks the cards you already have (In deck), and you can hide them.
  • The i on a card shows its details; ⟲ turns a double-faced card over.

Access code

Without it a simple built-in translator is used; with it, Claude understands you much better. Enter it under Settings.

Your other devices

Turn on Sync under Settings and enter the sync code on your other device. No account needed.

Import your deck

Paste your deck list, for example from Moxfield: Export → Copy for MTGO or Copy plain text. One card per line, like 1 Sol Ring (C21) 263 or Sol Ring. Results then mark the cards you already have.

Settings
