How to use Scryfall Zoekhulp

🔎 Searching

Describe the cards you want, like cheap removal in black or blue instants that draw a card. Colors, card types, mana value, price, keywords and what a card does all work. Dutch works too (with the access code). Only Commander-legal paper cards are shown. Already know Scryfall's syntax? Start with /s, e.g. /s t:elf mv=1.

👑 Your commander

Type your commander's name under the search bar and pick it from the suggestions. From then on every search only shows cards that fit in its colors. Click remove to search all colors again.

💬 Follow-up questions (access code)

After a search, type a change instead of starting over: only under 1 euro, without creatures, nu alleen instants.

♾️ Combos (access code)

Ask for combos, like 2 card combos in dimir, combos with Sol Ring or cheap combos in boros. The combos appear under the search with what they do and their price; click one to see only its cards.

🎛️ Filters

Above the results you can narrow them down by card type, mana value and price with one click.

📋 Your list and Moxfield

Click a card to add it to your list, click it again to remove it. Basic lands get a counter instead. Copy for Moxfield copies the list; paste it in Moxfield under Import or Bulk Edit. The list is saved in this browser.

📥 Import your deck

In the card list, Import deck takes your deck list (for example copied from Moxfield). Cards you already have get an In deck label, and you can hide them.

🕘 Recent searches

With the access code you see what others searched for on the right; your own searches are shared there too (without names).

🔑 Access code

Without the code a simple built-in translator is used. With it, Claude understands your searches much better. Ask the owner of this page for the code and enter it under ⚙ Settings.

Import your deck

Paste your deck list, for example from Moxfield: Export → Copy for MTGO or Copy plain text. One card per line, like 1 Sol Ring (C21) 263 or Sol Ring. Results then mark the cards you already have.
