How to use Scryfall Zoekhulp

Searching

Describe the cards you want, like cheap removal in black or blue instants that draw a card. Colors, card types, mana value, price, keywords and what a card does all work. Dutch works too (with the access code). Cheap means a low mana value (2 or less) and expensive a high one (6 or more); for price use budget (under €2) or an amount, like under 1 euro. Only Commander-legal paper cards are shown. Already know Scryfall's syntax? Start with /s, e.g. /s t:elf mv=1.

Your commander

Type your commander's name under the search bar and pick it from the suggestions. From then on every search only shows cards that fit in its colors. Ask for a color outside them and the explanation turns red to say it was left out. Click remove to search all colors again.

Follow-up questions (access code)

After a search, type a change instead of starting over: only under 1 euro, without creatures, nu alleen instants.

Combos (access code)

Ask for combos, like 2 card combos in dimir, combos with Sol Ring or budget combos in boros. The combos appear under the search with what they do and their price; click one to see only its cards.

Filters

Above the results you can narrow them down by card type, mana value and price with one click.

EDHREC recommendations

Set your commander and press EDHREC top cards: you get the cards most played with that commander on EDHREC, most played first, with the share of decks on each card ("in 85% of decks"). Basic lands are left out.

With the access code you can also type it. Use a word like recommended, edhrec, top, best, staples or aanbevolen, plus a commander or "my commander": recommended cards for Atraxa, edhrec Krenko, top ramp for my commander, aanbevolen removal voor Prosper. Without such a word (removal for my commander) you get a normal search within your commander's colors.

Cards that are already in your imported deck get the In deck label. Loading takes a few seconds.

Your list and Moxfield

Click a card to add it to your list, click it again to remove it. Basic lands get a counter instead. Copy for Moxfield copies the list; paste it in Moxfield under Import or Bulk Edit. Open it with List at the top (on a phone: the bar at the bottom). The list is saved in this browser. It is grouped by card type, with the mana curve of your spells at the top. The i on a card opens its details: rules text, price and links to Scryfall and EDHREC. Double-faced cards have a ⟲ button to turn them over.

Import your deck

In the card list, Import deck takes your deck list (copied from Moxfield, Archidekt, MTG Arena and the like). Cards you already have get an In deck label, and you can hide them.

Access code

Without the code a simple built-in translator is used. With it, Claude understands your searches much better. Ask the owner of this page for the code and enter it under Settings.

Import your deck

Paste your deck list, for example from Moxfield: Export → Copy for MTGO or Copy plain text. One card per line, like 1 Sol Ring (C21) 263 or Sol Ring. Results then mark the cards you already have.

Settings

Ask the owner of this page for the code. With it, Claude (claude-haiku-5-5) translates your searches; without it the built-in translator is used. The code is stored only in this browser on this device.
